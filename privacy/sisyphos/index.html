SISYPHOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Guinea Pig Games LLC ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game SISYPHOS (the "Game") available on Android through Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.

  • No accounts: The Game has no accounts of its own and does not ask for your name, email address, or other personal details.
  • No analytics or tracking: The Game does not use analytics tools or tracking software, and does not collect advertising IDs.
  • No advertising: The Game contains no advertisements.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The Game saves a small amount of data locally on your device so it is there the next time you play: your best score, your selected boulder skin and hat, and whether you own the Supporter Pack. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

3. Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following Google services. We do not receive personal information from them beyond what is needed for these features to work.

Google Play Billing

If you buy the optional Supporter Pack, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The Game checks with Google Play which items you own so it can unlock them and restore them after a reinstall.

Google Play Games Services

The Game uses Google Play Games Services for achievements. When you are signed in to Google Play Games, your sign-in and gameplay progress (such as unlocked achievements) are processed by Google.

Google's handling of your information is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

6. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to Guinea Pig Games LLC at guineapiggamesllc@gmail.com.
